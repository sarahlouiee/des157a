Best Practices for Form Design

October 6, 2026

This article by Salim Ansari touches on the best practices for form design. He goes over 20 of the best practices to facilitate the experience for the user, which incentivizes them to complete the entire thing instead of clicking off. A few things surprised me about this article -- for example, I had no idea that placeholder text hurts usability rather than helping it. I also wasn't aware that a one column option was more useful than a two column form. I took a business writing class last year, where we had to survey UC Davis students, and I used many of these tips, like keeping it short and the important questions at the top, but I definitely could have optimized the survey more.

He did bring up some tips that I never thought of too much, but always appreciated. For example, using matching keyboards helps a lot when inputting numbers, especially on mobile devices. If I need to enter my zip code or phone number, it is much easier for me to use a number pad instead of the tiny numbers at the top of my phone keyboard. Other things that I realized were helpful was always including a "Show password" button. As someone who types fast with varying accuracy, I always appreciate that button to ensure that I always type in my correct password.
