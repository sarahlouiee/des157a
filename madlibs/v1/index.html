UC Davis Day From Hell

MAD LIBS

Sarah begins their day by going to their first class at Walker Hall. Unfortunately, the Unitrans bus they were on had to be forcefully evacuated because a cow tried to hitch a ride. This left them stranded in the Arboretum, with no way to get to class except by hot air balloon.

After arriving late to class, they ate a baked potato with their friend, Taylor Swift. It was then that they were notified via carrier pigeon that they had to meet with Gary May urgently.

During the meeting, they learned that they had been expelled for dancing on top of the tables in class, which made their classmates feel confused.

Since this would be their last day at UC Davis, they decided to dance one last time on their way home.
